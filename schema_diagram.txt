KEY RELATIONSHIP SUMMARY:

1. financial_profiles.user_id → requests.user_id (1:many)
2. financial_profiles.user_id → financial_events.user_id (1:many)
3. financial_profiles.user_id → messages.user_id (1:many)
4. requests.request_id → request_payment_options.request_id (1:many, 2-4 options)
5. requests.request_id → messages.request_id (1:many, ~0.5 msg/request)
6. requests.request_id → images.request_id (1:0 or 1, only 16 total)
7. financial_events.event_id → images.related_event_id (1:0 or 1, only 16 total)
8. financial_events.event_id → messages.related_event_id (1:0 or 1, 39 total)
9. financial_events.event_id → financial_events.linked_event_id (self-ref, 58 pairs)
10. exchange_rates ↔ financial_events: on (settlement_date, currency) → home_currency
